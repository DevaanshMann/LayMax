Layover Maximizer Initial Prompt:

I have a layover at [AIRPORT] in [CITY].

I land at [ARRIVAL TIME] at [ARRIVAL TERMINAL], and my next flight departs at [DEPARTURE TIME] from [DEPARTURE TERMINAL].

Passport: [COUNTRY]. Checked bags: [YES / NO].

Budget: [AMOUNT]. Interests: [INTERESTS].

Calculate my usable free time by subtracting: deplaning, immigration and customs, travel to and from the city, security re-entry, and arriving at the gate [BUFFER] minutes before boarding.
Give a one word verdict: LEAVE, STAY, or RISKY, with a one line reason.
If LEAVE: build a timed itinerary with transport options, costs, and a bolded "Head back by" time.
If STAY or RISKY: suggest the best things to do inside the airport.
List anything I must verify before deciding (visa rules, transit schedules, terminal changes).
